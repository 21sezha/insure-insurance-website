Insure named among top insurtechs 2026

Published September 2026 • 4 mins read

Buying life insurance for the first time can feel overwhelming. There are dozens of plan types, confusing terms, and a lot of pressure to "get it right." Here's what actually matters before you sign anything.

1. Know how much coverage you actually need

A common ruleof thumb is 10-15 times your annual income, but your real number depends on your debts, dependents, and future goals. Don't just pick the biggest number available - pick the right one.

2. Understand the difference between term and whole life

Term life covers you for a set number of years and is usually cheaper. Whole life lasts your entire lifetime and builds cash value, but costs more. Most people starting out are better served by term.

3. Check what's excluded

Every policy has exclusions. Read the fine porint on what isn't covered so there are no surprises later

4. Compare more than just the price

The cheapest plan isn't always the best value. Look at claim payout speed, customer support, and reviews from real policyholders.

5. Revisit your plan as life changes

Getting married, having kids, buying a house - all of these are good moments to reassess whether your coverage still fits.
